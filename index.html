Welcome

Strathclyde Chancellor's Fellow · Department of Architecture, University of Strathclyde

Trained at the intersection of Architecture and Civil and Environmental Engineering, my research explores decarbonizing the built environment through energy retrofits right-sized for different occupant profiles. Currently serving as a Strathclyde Chancellor's Fellow, my work focuses on the integration of environmental and physiological data to reduce the energy gap in existing buildings and rethink the extent and scale of energy retrofits. I aim to move beyond one-size-fits-all efficiency measures toward frameworks that balance the energy performance of buildings, health, and wellbeing — advancing low-energy futures for the built environment.
