Welcome to My Academic Website

19 February 2026
news research

Introducing my new academic website — built to share research, publications, and thoughts on building decarbonization.

Welcome to my new academic website. I'm Carolina Recart, a Strathclyde Chancellor's Fellow at the University of Strathclyde in Glasgow, and this site is where I'll be sharing my research, publications, and occasional thoughts on building energy, decarbonization, and academic life.

What you'll find here

This site is organized around a few key areas:

  • Research — an overview of my current and past research themes, from hygrothermal performance to occupant behavior and fuel poverty
  • Publications — a full, up-to-date list of journal articles, conference papers, and patents
  • Projects — summaries of funded research projects I've led or contributed to
  • Teaching — courses I've taught and students I've supervised
  • CV — a downloadable version of my full academic CV

What I work on

My research sits at the intersection of architecture and engineering. I'm interested in what actually happens when we retrofit buildings for energy efficiency — not just in the models, but in real buildings occupied by real people.

That means asking questions like:

  • When we add insulation and seal up a building, are we inadvertently creating moisture problems?
  • How do people's actual heating behaviors differ from what we assume in our energy models?
  • Who benefits from energy retrofit programs, and who gets left behind?

These questions connect engineering practice to social equity and climate justice, which is what makes the field so interesting to work in.
